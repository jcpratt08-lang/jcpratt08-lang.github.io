Paul privacy policy

Paul is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar to read, summarize, draft, send and trash email and to read calendar events, only at the owner's request.

Data is processed on the owner's private server and sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Paul's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
